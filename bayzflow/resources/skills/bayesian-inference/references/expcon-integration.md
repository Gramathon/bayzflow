# EXPCON integration

EXPCON owns experiment definition, freeze, execution admission, evidence collection, verification and adjudication. Declare Bayzflow source and dependency identities, model/data inputs, guide and selected-layer configuration, seeds, metrics and posterior tensor outputs in the EXPCON contract. Have the trusted executor invoke Bayzflow, then include artifact identifiers and SHA256 checksums in collected evidence. Do not treat Bayzflow output metrics as their own acceptance criteria.
